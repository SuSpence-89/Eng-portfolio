# Spencer Holmes, CD, C.E.T.

### Mechanical Design | Engineering Technology | Aviation Systems

Welcome to my professional engineering technology portfolio.

I am a Certified Engineering Technologist (C.E.T.) with a background
spanning mechanical design, aircraft maintenance, technical inspection,
equipment troubleshooting, fabrication, and renewable energy systems.

My career began in the Royal Canadian Air Force (RCAF), where I gained
extensive hands-on experience maintaining aviation mechanical systems,
conducting component inspections, performing functional testing, and
working within regulated airworthiness frameworks.

Building on this technical foundation, I pursued formal engineering
technology education and transitioned into mechanical design and
engineering-focused work.

My interests centre on practical engineering, mechanical systems,
computer-aided design, prototyping, and solving real-world technical
challenges.
