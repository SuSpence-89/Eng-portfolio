## About LRDL Engineering and Rail Shop Services

LRDL Engineering and Rail Shop Services work together in Edmonton to design, build, and maintain railway facilities and equipment. Their work includes locomotive repair shops, wheel shops, drop tables, sanding systems, and fueling stations.

## Portable Hydraulic Power Unit

During my time with Rail Shop Services, I worked on a portable hydraulic power unit. The sample design concept below illustrates how the components can fit on a mobile frame. Casters allow the unit to be rolled into position. The lower frame carries the motor and hydraulic reservoir, while the upper deck keeps the pressure gauge, controls, and hose within reach.
